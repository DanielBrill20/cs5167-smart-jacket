# Smart Jacket Design Properties

## Affordances

- Wearable
- Openable/Closeable (via buttons or zippers)
- Washable (could be an anti-affordance with a smart jacket)
- Throwable, foldable, etc.

## Physical Properties

**Properties:**
- Medium sized
- Portable
- Medium weight
- Soft

**Has:**
- Sleeves
- Pockets

**Could have:**
- Buttons
- Zippers
- A hood

## Signifiers
- Care label (tells you how to wash)

## Assumptions
- Capacitance touch buttons
- Jacket back is a screen
- Can sense movement
- Can sense temperature
